# Welcome to Nexus Docs

This is a starter page. **Edit `content/index.md`** to replace it with your own welcome content.

## What you can do here

- Write Markdown or MDX — files under `content/` become pages
- Gate pages by tier with the `access_tier` frontmatter field
- Group pages into product/extension hierarchies
- Embed videos with `<VideoEmbed src="..." />`
- Highlight callouts with `<Admonition>`
- Render extension grids with `<ExtensionGrid path="..." />`
